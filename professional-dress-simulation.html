Professional Dress Challenge

Choose a character, explore a situation, and build an outfit. Dress for the setting, not for a stereotype.

1. Choose your character and setting

All clothing choices are available to either character.

2. Dress your character

Drag an illustrated item onto the character, or tap/click it to put it on. Choose a top, bottom, and shoes. Dresses and one-piece outfits fill both top and bottom.

3. Review your outfit

Choose your clothing to begin.

There is no grade or submission. Clothing expectations vary by organization, role, culture, accessibility needs, and safety rules. Follow the specific instructions provided by your employer or institution.
