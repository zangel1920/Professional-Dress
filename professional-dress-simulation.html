COLS 1200 | MODULE 3 | UNGRADED PRACTICE

Professional Dress Challenge

Choose a character, select a situation, and build an appropriate outfit.

1. Choose Your Character

All clothing options are available for either character.

2. Choose Your Clothing

Drag clothing onto the character or click an item. Choose a top, bottom, and shoes. One-piece outfits replace the top and bottom.

3. Review Your Outfit

Choose your clothing to begin.

This activity is ungraded. No submission is required. Professional clothing depends on the setting, safety requirements, and organizational expectations.
